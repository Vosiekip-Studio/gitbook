# Can I use my own domain for my Tebex store?

**Short answer:** yes, but on Tebex itself a custom domain needs a paid **Tebex Plus** subscription. For free, your store runs on a Tebex address such as `mynetwork.tebex.io`. Another way is a **Tebex website**: your own website on your own domain, with payments still handled by Tebex.

***

### The free Tebex address

Every Tebex store gets a free address that ends in `.tebex.io`, for example `mynetwork.tebex.io`. You can change the first part in the Tebex Creator Panel under **Webstore → Domain**.

### A custom domain with Tebex Plus

To use your own domain, such as `store.mynetwork.com`, directly on Tebex, you need a **Tebex Plus** subscription. You then add a **CNAME record** at your domain provider that points to Tebex. If the subscription ends, the custom domain stops working. See [Tebex's own guide](https://docs.tebex.io/creators/tebex-control-panel/webstore/domains/subdomain-and-custom-domain) for the steps.

### Your own Tebex website

A Tebex website runs on your own hosting and your own domain. Your packages, payments and wallet stay in Tebex, and the website shows them in your own design, with its own admin panel.

* **You need:** hosting that runs Node.js and a domain. Both are cheap, see [Choosing your hosting](../tebex-website-guides/installation-guide.md) and [Setting up your domain](../tebex-website-guides/setting-up-your-domain.md).
* **How to install:** see our [Tebex website installation guide →](../tebex-website-guides/installation-guide.md).

***

### Which one should I pick?

| You want…                                     | Pick                        |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| No extra costs                                | The free `.tebex.io` address |
| Your domain, but no hosting to manage         | Tebex Plus                  |
| Your domain, your own design and admin panel  | **Tebex website**           |
