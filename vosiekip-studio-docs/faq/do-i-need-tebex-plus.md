# Do I need Tebex Plus?

**Short answer:** only if you want a **custom domain** or **custom templates (themes)** directly on Tebex. Gift cards, coupons, sales and unlimited packages are already included in the free Starter plan, and the platform fee is the same on both.

***

### What Plus adds

According to [Tebex's pricing page](https://docs.tebex.io/creators/pricing-overview/starter-and-plus-plans), Plus costs **£12.49 per month** (cheaper if you pay for a year) and adds:

* A **custom domain**, such as `store.mynetwork.com`.
* **Custom templates** with HTML and CSS editing.
* **Customer lookup**.

### What the free Starter plan already has

Unlimited packages, payment processing, seller protection, analytics, sales, coupons, **gift cards**, abandoned basket recovery and team accounts.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Custom templates are currently **not available on new stores**, even with Plus. See [Can I use a Tebex theme on a new Tebex store?](can-i-use-a-tebex-theme-on-a-new-tebex-store.md)
{% endhint %}

### Want a custom store without Plus?

* Run a **Tebex website** on your own domain, see [Tebex theme or Tebex website?](tebex-theme-or-tebex-website.md)
* Or use **PayNow**, which includes custom themes on its free plan, see [Tebex or PayNow?](tebex-or-paynow.md)

Prices change, so check Tebex's pricing page before you decide.
