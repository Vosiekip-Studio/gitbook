# What should a crystal PvP arena look like?

**Short answer:** **big and open**, with room for explosions and a floor players can place obsidian on. Keep it separate from the safe zone, and protect the build around it so explosions don't damage your spawn.

***

### Tips

* **Lots of space.** End crystals explode, and players need room to move. A small arena gets messy fast.
* **Open sky above.** Players use height in crystal PvP. A low ceiling gets in the way.
* **Separate from the safe zone.** Explosions shouldn't reach players who don't want to fight.
* **Protect the build.** Use a region plugin so explosions can't break your spawn, and let the arena reset if players change it. See [protecting your spawn](how-to-protect-your-spawn-from-griefing.md).
* **Easy to reach.** Players should get from the spawn point to the arena in seconds.

### Economy for crystal PvP

Players need obsidian, crystals and gear. A shop made for crystal PvP keeps prices fair, see [How do I balance shop prices?](how-to-balance-shop-prices.md)
