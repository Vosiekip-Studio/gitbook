# How do I add quests to my server?

**Short answer:** install a quest plugin and add quests in **categories**, such as mining, farming and combat. Give rewards that feel worth it, and add enough quests that players never run out.

***

### What makes good quests

* **Variety.** Mining, farming, fishing, combat and exploring, so every player finds something they like.
* **Categories,** so players find quests that fit their playstyle.
* **A steady curve,** from quick early quests to big long-term goals.
* **Good rewards,** such as money, keys or cosmetics.
* **Lots of quests.** Players finish quests faster than you expect.
