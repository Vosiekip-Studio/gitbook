# How do I add custom enchantments?

**Short answer:** install **CrazyEnchantments**, a popular custom enchantments plugin. It adds new enchants that players get from an enchanter menu, books or tinkering. Balance them so they're fun without making PvP unfair.

***

### How it works

* Players get custom enchants from **books**, which they apply to tools, weapons and armor.
* Enchants come in **tiers**, from common to very rare.
* Enchants can do things vanilla can't, such as extra damage effects or auto-smelting.

### Keep it fair

* **Not too strong.** Very powerful enchants make PvP one-sided.
* **Rare means rare.** The best enchants should take real effort to get.
* **Don't sell powerful enchants** for real money, see [making money without pay-to-win](how-to-make-money-without-pay-to-win.md).
