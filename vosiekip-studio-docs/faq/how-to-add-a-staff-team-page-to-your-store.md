# How do I add a staff team page to my store?

**Short answer:** all our themes and Tebex websites include a **Staff Team Page** add-on. Create a page in your store, match the title in the add-on, then fill in your staff roles and usernames.

***

### Why a staff page

Players trust a server more when they can see who runs it. A staff page also shows who to contact for help.

### How to add one

1. Create a page in your store (in Tebex: [creator.tebex.io/pages](https://creator.tebex.io/pages)).
2. Turn on the **Staff Team Page** add-on and type the page title **exactly** as you named the page.
3. Enter your role names (for example Owner, Admin, Moderator, Helper) and the usernames for each role.

Page not showing? See [Why is my staff page not showing?](why-is-my-store-page-not-showing.md)
