# How do I upgrade spawners on my server?

**Short answer:** use a spawner plugin such as **AxSpawners**. It lets players stack and upgrade spawners through a menu, for example to spawn faster or drop more.

***

### Why use a spawner plugin

* **Stacking.** Many spawners in one block, which saves space and lag.
* **Upgrades.** Players spend money to make spawners better, which gives them a long-term goal.
* **Spawners for every mob,** not only the ones you find in the world.

### Keep your economy in check

Spawner farms make a lot of money and items. Balance upgrade costs and drop prices, see [How do I balance shop prices?](how-to-balance-shop-prices.md) and [jobs without breaking your economy](how-to-set-up-jobs-without-breaking-your-economy.md).
