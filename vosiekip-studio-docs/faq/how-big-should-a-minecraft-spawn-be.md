# How big should a Minecraft server spawn be?

**Short answer:** for a **lifesteal** server, go big: our lifesteal spawns are **501×501 blocks**, so players have room to fight and crystal PvP without leaving spawn. Survival and SMP servers can use a smaller spawn, since players mostly pass through on their way to the wild.

Your spawn is the first thing new players see. Too small and it feels empty and cramped, too big and players get lost.

***

### Why lifesteal spawns need more room

On a lifesteal server, players lose a heart when they die and steal one when they win, so fighting is the main activity. A good lifesteal spawn has:

* **A safe zone** around the spawn point, where nobody can fight.
* **An arena** for PvP and **crystal PvP**. End crystals explode, so the arena needs open space.
* **Crates, shops and portals** that players reach quickly from the spawn point.

All of that needs space, which is why we build our lifesteal spawns at 501×501.

### Typical sizes per server type

| Server type     | Spawn size                    | Why                                                  |
| --------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Lifesteal       | Large, around 500×500         | Room for a safe zone, PvP and crystal PvP arenas.    |
| Survival / SMP  | Small to medium               | Players walk through it on their way to the wild.    |
| Hub / lobby     | Depends on your game modes    | Only for walking around and picking a game mode.     |

### Tips

* **List your features first.** Crates, shops, leaderboards, arenas and portals all need space.
* **Keep paths short.** From the spawn point, players should reach the arena, shops and portals quickly.
* **Think about performance.** Big, detailed builds have more to load, which matters on small servers.
