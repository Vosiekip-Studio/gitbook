# How do I balance shop prices on my server?

**Short answer:** make players **sell for less than they buy**, price items by how hard they are to get, and check for **money loops** where players can buy, craft and sell for a profit. Then watch your economy and adjust.

***

### Rules of thumb

* **Sell price lower than buy price.** Otherwise players buy and sell the same item for free money.
* **Price by effort.** Easy-to-farm items should be cheap, rare items expensive.
* **Watch for money loops.** Check that crafting a bought item and selling the result never makes a profit.
* **Limit the most farmable items,** or players with big farms take over the economy.
* **Adjust over time.** Watch what players sell most and change prices when needed.

### Crystal PvP economy

On crystal PvP servers, players use up obsidian, crystals and gear fast. Those items should be easy to get, or players stop fighting.
