# Can I switch from Oraxen to Nexo without losing my models?

**Short answer:** yes. Nexo's setup is close to Oraxen's, so switching is easy. For our model packs it's even simpler: every download already includes a ready-made **Nexo** folder, so you just install that one.

***

### Switching with our model packs

1. Open the model download and go to `0. Configs/Nexo`.
2. Follow the [Nexo installation guide →](../model-guides/nexo.md).

You don't need to buy anything again: the ItemsAdder, Nexo and Oraxen versions are all in the same download.

### Switching other content

Your own items and other packs need to be moved over too. Nexo's configs are similar to Oraxen's, but some settings have new names. See [Nexo's docs](https://docs.nexomc.com/) for the differences.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Back up first.** Copy your whole `plugins/Oraxen` folder to your computer before you switch.
{% endhint %}

Not sure which plugin to use? See [ItemsAdder, Nexo or Oraxen?](itemsadder-vs-nexo-vs-oraxen.md)
