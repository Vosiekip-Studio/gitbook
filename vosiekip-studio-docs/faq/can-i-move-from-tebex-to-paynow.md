# Can I move from Tebex to PayNow?

**Short answer:** yes. You set up your PayNow store next to your Tebex store, recreate your packages, connect your server, and switch your links once everything works. Your old Tebex purchases stay in Tebex.

***

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Set up PayNow next to Tebex

Keep your Tebex store online while you build the PayNow store, so you don't miss any sales. See [How do I set up a PayNow store?](how-to-set-up-a-paynow-store.md)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Recreate your packages

Add your ranks, keys and other products in PayNow, with the same commands as in Tebex. Copy the package images too.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Install a theme

Give your new store a design, see [How do I change the look of my PayNow store?](how-to-change-the-look-of-your-paynow-store.md)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Test and switch

Make a test purchase and check it arrives on your server. Then update the store link on your Discord, website and in-game `/buy` command, and remove the Tebex plugin.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Subscriptions** (monthly ranks) don't move by themselves. Tell your subscribers before you switch, so they can re-subscribe on PayNow.
{% endhint %}
