# How do I reward players for playtime?

**Short answer:** add a **playtime rewards menu** with tiers that unlock after a number of hours played. Players claim each reward in the menu, and there is always a next tier to work towards.

***

### What makes good playtime rewards

* **Quick first rewards,** after the first hour or two, so new players feel rewarded right away.
* **Long-term goals,** for players with hundreds or thousands of hours.
* **Rewards that grow** with each tier: money, keys, cosmetics and titles.
* **A clear menu** that shows what's claimed, what's next and how long to go.

{% hint style="info" %}
Playtime rewards are great for keeping players. See [How do I keep players coming back?](how-to-keep-players-coming-back.md)
{% endhint %}
