# How do I make money with my Minecraft server without being pay-to-win?

**Short answer:** sell things that **don't give a competitive advantage**, such as cosmetics, ranks with cosmetic or quality-of-life perks, and server-wide rewards. Mojang's rules don't allow purchases that ruin other players' experience or give paying players an edge.

***

### What Mojang's guidelines say

Minecraft servers must follow the [Minecraft Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines) and the [EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula). In short:

* **Cosmetics are allowed**, except capes or anything that looks like a cape.
* **Gameplay perks are allowed** only if they don't ruin other players' experience or give a competitive advantage.
* **A virtual currency is allowed** if it has no real-world value, can't be cashed out, and doesn't look like Minecoins.
* **Everything must suit all ages**, so no gambling.
* **Show your prices and what players get** before they buy.

{% hint style="warning" %}
This is a summary, not legal advice. Read the guidelines yourself, they change from time to time.
{% endhint %}

### What you can sell

* **Cosmetic ranks:** a chat prefix, name color, particles, hats.
* **Quality of life:** extra homes, `/nick`, more vaults, kits that don't help in PvP.
* **Cosmetics:** hats, pets, trails, see [cosmetics like hats](how-to-add-cosmetics-like-hats-to-your-server.md).
* **Server-wide rewards:** a community goal that unlocks a reward for everyone.

### Be careful with

* **Random rewards for money**, such as paid crate keys with powerful items. They come close to gambling and pay-to-win.
* **Anything that helps in PvP** or on leaderboards.
