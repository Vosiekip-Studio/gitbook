# Tebex or PayNow: which should I use?

**Short answer:** both are solid webstores built for game servers. **Tebex** is the long-time standard. **PayNow** has lower platform fees and allows custom themes on its free plan. If a custom design matters to you and your Tebex store is new, PayNow is currently the easier choice.

***

### Quick comparison

|                          | Tebex                                                         | PayNow                                                        |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Platform fee             | 5% (15% for FiveM)                                            | 3.5% on the free plan, lower on paid plans                    |
| Custom themes            | Needs **Tebex Plus**, and not available on new stores right now | Included on the free plan                                     |
| Custom domain            | Needs **Tebex Plus**                                          | Check PayNow's plans                                          |
| Gift cards, coupons, sales | Included                                                    | Included                                                      |

Payment provider fees (such as PayPal or cards) come on top for both.

{% hint style="info" %}
Tebex's numbers are from [Tebex's pricing page](https://docs.tebex.io/creators/pricing-overview/starter-and-plus-plans). PayNow's numbers are from [PayNow's own comparison page](https://paynow.gg/compare/tebex). Fees change, so check both before you decide.
{% endhint %}

### Pick Tebex if…

* You already run a Tebex store and are happy with it.
* Your players are used to Tebex checkout.
* You want a fully custom store on your own domain: use a [Tebex website](tebex-theme-or-tebex-website.md).

### Pick PayNow if…

* You're starting a new store and want a custom theme right away.
* Lower platform fees matter to you.

Switching later? See [Can I move from Tebex to PayNow?](can-i-move-from-tebex-to-paynow.md)
