# How do I keep players coming back to my server?

**Short answer:** give players a reason to log in **every day** (quests, a battle pass) and a reason to stay **for months** (skills, playtime rewards, long-term goals). Reward progress, and players keep coming back.

***

### Daily reasons to log in

* **Daily quests,** see [adding quests](how-to-add-quests-to-your-server.md).
* **A battle pass** with daily and weekly challenges, see [adding a battle pass](how-to-add-a-battle-pass.md).
* **Daily rewards** for logging in.

### Long-term reasons to stay

* **Skills** that level up, see [mcMMO or AuraSkills?](mcmmo-or-auraskills.md)
* **Playtime rewards** for hundreds of hours, see [rewarding playtime](how-to-reward-players-for-playtime.md).
* **Jobs** and an **economy** worth taking part in.

### Community

Events, a Discord, leaderboards and a friendly staff team matter just as much as plugins.
