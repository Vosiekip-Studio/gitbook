# Where do I get skyblock islands for my server?

**Short answer:** you can build them yourself, or buy **ready-made islands** as schematics. A good skyblock server has small **starter islands** for new players and bigger islands, such as **farming islands**, to explore and unlock.

***

### What to look for

* **Starter islands** that are small but have everything a player needs to begin.
* **Themed islands**, such as farming, mining or nether islands, to give players goals.
* **Schematic files**, so you can paste them or use them in your skyblock plugin.
* **Your Minecraft version**, see [using a build on another version](can-i-use-a-build-on-another-minecraft-version.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
Skyblock plugins each have their own way of loading island schematics. Check your plugin's docs to see where island files go.
{% endhint %}

Starting a whole skyblock server? See [How do I start a Hypixel-style skyblock server?](how-to-start-a-hypixel-style-skyblock-server.md)
