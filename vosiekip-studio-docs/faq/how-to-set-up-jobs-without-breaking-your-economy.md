# How do I set up jobs without breaking my server's economy?

**Short answer:** install **JobsReborn** with **Vault** and an economy plugin, then **balance** the pay so jobs are worth it but don't flood your server with money. Turn off pay for farms and spawners that players can abuse.

***

### What you need

* **JobsReborn**, the jobs plugin.
* **Vault** and an **economy plugin**, such as EssentialsX. Without them, JobsReborn can't pay players.

### Keep your economy healthy

* **Don't overpay.** If players earn too fast, prices in shops lose their meaning.
* **Stop farm abuse.** Turn off pay for mobs from spawners and for blocks players place and break again.
* **Protect spawn.** Don't pay for actions in spawn or other protected areas.
* **Pick a sensible max level.** A very high max level is never reached and feels pointless.

See also [How do I balance shop prices?](how-to-balance-shop-prices.md)
